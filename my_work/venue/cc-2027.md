### Call for papers — key passages, verbatim

Papers page:

> Papers report substantial, original contributions to the study and support of creativity. We welcome work spanning **creativity-support tools and environments**, studies that capture and analyze creative processes, theoretical and empirical reflection, methods for evaluation, and creativity across diverse cultural and disciplinary contexts. Submissions are especially encouraged to engage the 2027 theme.

Pictorials page:

> Pictorials communicate their contribution primarily through visual means such as diagrams, photographs, illustrations, and annotated designs, with text playing a supporting role. They suit work where the **craft, form, and materiality** of an idea are best shown rather than described.

> Visuals must be primary: content should be at most half text; text-heavy work is better suited to the Papers track.
