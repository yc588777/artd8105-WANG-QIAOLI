## 海棠經緯

**HAITANG WEAVE LAB**

汪俏黎 / Wang Qiaoli

海棠经纬把竹团扇写成一套可交付的编织文法：扇框锁定于母版。通过图案选择和参数化调整，可实时生成以经纬波线相穿的扇面。平面、三维与工艺图共用这一次序。手艺人可据此备篾、入框、穿编；设计师调整疏密、方向与弧度，制作标准同步更新。海棠经纬让设计师与竹编手艺人面对同一份可执行的方案。

"Haitang Jingwei" translates the craft of making round bamboo-strip fans into a structured, executable weaving methodology, with the fan frame serving as a master template. By selecting patterns and adjusting parameters, one can generate—in real time—a fan surface composed of interwoven, undulating warp and weft strips. This unified system generates 2D layouts, 3D models, and technical production drawings simultaneously. Artisans can follow these specifications to prepare bamboo strips, fit them into the frame, and execute the weaving, meanwhile, designers can fine-tune the density, orientation, and curvature of the weave, with the production standards updating automatically. In this way, "Haitang Jingwei" provides both designers and bamboo-weaving artisans with a shared, actionable blueprint.
