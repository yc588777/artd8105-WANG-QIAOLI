## Territory

I keep circling living systems that stay dark until they are disturbed, and then emit a short light that is not drawn on afterwards. In the night-sea work that disturbance is a path, a pattern, or a body; the glow is *Pyrocystis* switching on, not a beam. In the computer-art reconstruction that disturbance is also a written lyric becoming phoneme cards, and a camera becoming pitch and tract. What I am circling is authorship as the writing of a system that can still surprise me when something live enters it.

## The rung

**Live input** — after rule-in-2D.

The algorithm explorer was rung one: I specify the rule, the picture stays inside the rule. I have already done that. The next position I am taking is live input: a camera or a body is allowed to rewrite parameters I did not keyframe. In HYBRID.06, motion changes every visual layer and also PITCH, DUR and TRACT. In *Fluorescent Tide*, the body-wake mode uses the camera as capture, not as a picture of the room. I am not claiming learned models. I am claiming that my authorship is the constraint of the system, not every frame.

## What you would make

The smallest version that would still surprise me: a dark field of pyrocystis that stays almost unlit until a live body or a spoken line disturbs it, and the disturbance is visible as glow and audible as a changing tract, not as a UI overlay.

## The pairing sentence

I am going to make a live pyrocystis field that can also speak a punched-card lyric, using a browser system with camera motion and Kelly–Gerstman tract controls, and it would tell someone in generative art (and, if I keep the CHI thread, in HCI) that light and voice here are a living response to disturbance, not an effect drawn on top of the picture.
