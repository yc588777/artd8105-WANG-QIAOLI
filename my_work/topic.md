## Territory

I keep circling living systems that stay dark until they are disturbed, then emit a short light that is not painted on afterwards. In *Fluorescent Tide* that disturbance is a path, a pattern, or a body; the glow is *Pyrocystis* switching on. In the Session 4 reconstruction that disturbance is also a written lyric becoming phoneme cards, and a camera becoming pitch and tract. What I am circling is authorship as the writing of a system that can still surprise me when something live enters it.

## The rung

**Live input** — after rule-in-2D.

The brief’s ladder is: rule in 2D → rule in 3D → external data → live input → capture → learned models. I have already done rung one: the algorithm explorer specifies the rule, and the picture stays inside it. The next position I am taking is live input. A camera or a body is allowed to rewrite parameters I did not keyframe. In HYBRID.06, motion changes every visual layer and also PITCH, DUR and TRACT. In *Fluorescent Tide*, body-wake uses the camera as capture, not as a picture of the room. I am not claiming learned models. Authorship here is the constraint of the system, not every frame.
